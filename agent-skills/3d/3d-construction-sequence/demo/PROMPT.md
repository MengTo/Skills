## Minimal prompt

Use $3d-construction-sequence to assemble a ferry terminal one structural part at a time.

## Recreate the demo

Start with the water and distant shore. Seat the wharf, then the columns, enclosed bays, roof trusses, roof finish and beacon. Each rigid part has one start time and displacement shared by its opaque surface and wire cage. Let its cyan edges appear just ahead of the solid, warm briefly on contact and fade. A horizontal truss must hold its length and a diagonal brace must stay straight. Give the viewer deterministic scrubbing and replay. Let the entrance and roof cameras inspect intermediate states. Use keyboard-accessible controls and a static opening frame for reduced motion.
