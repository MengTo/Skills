## Minimal prompt

Use $3d-feature-objects. Model a shelf of feature illustrations that turn under the pointer.

## Recreate the demo

Build three responsive HTML cards for a field instrument collection. Model a depth gauge with an enamel face, a flow meter with a three-blade impeller, and a sampling probe with exposed metal guards. Give each model a composed starting angle and its own pivot. Place the three objects under the same studio lights. Hover over a card to start a slow turn, move horizontally to steer it, and ease back to the resting angle on exit. Use one WebGL renderer and update only visible cards whose state changes. Keep the card headings, descriptions, units and turn buttons in HTML. Check the render at narrow widths and after holding one card for two full turns.
