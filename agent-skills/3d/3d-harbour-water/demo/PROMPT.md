# Night water

## Minimal prompt

Use $3d-harbour-water to give this Three.js quay moving water that reflects the lamps and the terminal. Float the harbour launch at its actual waterline and carry its wake around turns.

## Recreate the demo

Build a sheltered working harbour at blue hour. Put the camera near a timber landing, looking over the bow of a small pilot launch toward a row of bonded stores. Place a cyan terminal sign and warm sodium lamps along the far bank. Their broken reflections should start beneath the actual sources and the near piles should meet their reflections. Animate long shallow swells beneath shorter ripples, with seeded rain rings close enough to see. Fit the launch to the wave heights at its four corners. Give it a curved route and leave a wake in the water it has passed through. Include calm, rain and squall controls, a waterline camera and a pause button. Show a composed still frame for reduced motion.

## Remix prompt

Move the technique to a narrow Amsterdam canal at dawn. Use brick bridges, houseboats and warm apartment windows as the reflection sources. Preserve the contact point under each pier and compare the water from a bridge and a mooring before tuning the small ripples.
