# Kasumidera visual field study

## Minimal prompt

Use $3d-kasumidera-ultra-realism to art-direct a walk from a vermilion torii across a warm timber bridge. Preserve my opening camera. Compare the lacquer, hinoki and near rock at 200% before you score the pass.

## Recreate the demo

Collect six frames from a finished walkthrough. Use the arrival at the torii to discuss composition, the underside of a temple roof for joinery, the bridge approach for warm timber, a near ridge for rock relief, a cloud bank for depth and an open journal for paper. Put the frames on a quiet contact sheet and let each one open at a larger size. Write one observation that a reader can verify directly in each frame. Offer a detail crop centered on the relevant surface and keyboard navigation between frames. Keep the source aspect ratio throughout. Evaluate parallax, occlusion and page-turn behavior in the moving game.

## Remix prompt

Apply the same review to a coastal monastery in the rain. Pick a protected arrival view, find a single nearby material that breaks at walking distance and make a second frame after changing it. Keep sunlight, focal length and exposure fixed so the reader can judge the change.
