# Prompt the next pass

These are reusable prompts distilled from the creator's requests. Substitute the setting, protected view and palette for another game. For the original image-generation wording, open [the model prompt archive](generation-prompts.md).

### Start from a walkable shot

> Build a walkable Three.js sanctuary above the clouds. Stage the opening at player height beneath a vermilion torii. Let worn stone steps lead to a pagoda and a timber bridge. Use the painterly reference for the masses of warm light and cool mountain shadow. Give every near silhouette physical depth, including the space under the eaves and below the bridge. Return a live entrance frame, a side view from the ravine and a short walk between them.

The original brief assigned different jobs to its three public references: technology, painterly autumn art direction and game-environment inspiration. Save that assignment with the brief so a lighting iteration preserves the game camera.

### Generate a target and isolated specimens

> Use the current gate frame as the image reference for Seedream 5.0 Pro. Hold the frame edges, gate opening, stair width, roof position and sun direction. Improve chipped wood, cut stone, backlit leaves and valley depth. For a Seedream 5.5 maple specimen, put the complete face-on leaf and petiole inside the square. Keep the light neutral and the background transparent. Check the alpha edge before placing it on a folded blade.

### Refine one weak object

> At 200% optical scale, the closest mountain looks round and the foreground column looks too smooth. First capture each in grazing light. Give the rock one readable ledge and a fracture that continues around a corner. On the column, break the lacquer highlight where fine chips expose cedar. Keep the current warm colors. Return the two matched close-ups and the unchanged entrance framing.

> Rebuild the bridge from the boards down. Follow the camber through the bearers, ribs and stone bearings. Keep the existing honey-colored hinoki and red rails. Check forward, reverse and side views, then cross at eye height. Restore the torii as the opening view when the bridge inspection is finished.

> The temple must hold up when I walk beneath its roof. Model the bays, brackets, rafter tails, ceramic courses, lattice and door depth from scratch. Tell me which other assets came from image-to-3D and which pieces you replaced. Show an under-eave view with daylight reaching the cut ends.

### Undo the color while keeping a useful improvement

> Compare the same four positions in the before and after frames. Restore the honey-colored bridge, ochre steps, brown maple bark and green fir. Keep improved joints, fractures, needle geometry and grain direction wherever they work with the original color. Tell me exactly which changes survived.

### Turn three UI concepts into three states

> Give me three Seedream 5.0 Pro studies from the same entrance. Use the minimal one for exploration, the topographic map when I ask for directions, and the journal before movement begins. Keep type small and legible. The journal itself is a textured 3D object with a hinged cover, two-sided leaves, worn paper, real ink impressions and weighted bookmarks.

> I turned the page and it animated back. A committed drag must land on the selected spread and stay there even if the last pointer coordinate slips back. Keep short nudges reversible. Test forward, backward and lost capture. Let me click either page, turn with buttons or arrows, and click the scenery to exit. Keep ribbons clear of the paper.

### Review and record

> Work toward 9/10 using the same camera, sun and exposure. Score composition, light, materials and fine detail. Fix the largest visible loss and compare the original, best and newest frames. If the candidate hides the pagoda or changes the approved color, carry forward only the parts that improve the protected shot.

> Add music, environmental ambience and surface-specific footsteps. Use sounds in the supplied library and Earshot for selection or measurements when available. Record the revised 40-second walkthrough at native 3840 × 2160 and 60 fps with stereo audio. Hold the journal opening long enough to see the paper settle. Verify the opening aspect ratio and audio alignment.

> Prepare a tutorial handoff with the reference, the actual game captures, prompts, model choices, credit lines, dates, reversions, shot times and verification results. Supply the requested HTML or React source and compact review video alongside the master when those are part of the brief.

For a new location, replace the torii with its entrance landmark, hinoki with its local material, and the shrine seal with an appropriate interactive object. Preserve the evaluation method and let the architecture change.
