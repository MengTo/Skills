## Minimal prompt

Use $3d-modular-buildings to model a ferry terminal that reads from the water, the landing and the roof.

## Recreate the demo

Make one structural bay the unit of the design. Show a sheltered public entrance, separate service end, actual piles under the deck, a short flight of landing steps and a beacon supported by the end tower. Model the trusses, ridge, gutter, projecting sills, recessed windows and door opening in three dimensions. Use repeated spans with a deliberate break at the entrance. Let a warm occupied bay guide the eye across the cooler iron and concrete. Provide three camera buttons so the viewer can inspect proportions and attachment points from the water, the entrance and the roof.

## Remix prompt

Reuse the same bay kit for a fish market. Replace the beacon silhouette with a cold-storage vent stack and give the loading side a wider opening and protected delivery apron.
