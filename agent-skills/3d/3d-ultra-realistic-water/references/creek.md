# Moving water in a shallow creek

Use this branch for a creek where viewers can see the bed and walk around individual stones. The ocean module in this skill covers water with an open horizon. The smaller example lives in [creek-optics.mjs](../assets/creek-optics.mjs). Its functions accept metres and seconds.

1. Draw the wetted channel from a centreline, bank widths and bed elevations. Use one height function for the edges, floating leaves, wet lines on rocks and cascade lips. At each texel record the bed depth and the signed distance to the nearest obstacle. Keep dry islands out of the water mesh.
2. Start velocity tangent to the centreline. Deflect it around an exposed rock by removing the component entering the surface. The example uses potential flow around a cylinder. Its velocity is tangential at the rock and approaches the input velocity at distance. Blend local corrections across a small neighbourhood if several stones overlap. Add a slower, broken wake downstream. Transport foam and normal-map phases in the same coordinate system so foam carries past a stationary stone.
3. Render opaque surfaces to a colour and depth target first. Reconstruct the bed hit from depth. Refract in view space and reject a displaced sample if its solid surface is above the water or in front of the entry point. Fall back to the undistorted bed in that case. Attenuate the three linear-light channels independently using the underwater path length. Farther stones lose warm detail first. Close dry rock edges should retain their silhouettes.
4. Reflect the camera across the water plane and clip the opposite half-space. Keep water and spray out of the reflection pass. Weight the result by the surface normal and the eye direction. At normal incidence freshwater reflects about 2%. At a grazing angle the reflected bank should dominate. Momiji Creek uses two water-level probes for rays that leave the reflected image, with radial depth to correct parallax. Place probes in open water and refresh them when lighting presets change.
5. At a ledge, use separate ribbons with different widths and gaps. Derive the horizontal travel and impact point from lip velocity and gravitational drop. Place whitewater and spray at that impact. Give bright foam a short lifetime. Keep quiet upstream pools transparent.
6. Verify the creek from a low grazing angle, above the channel, and beside a wet rock. Change from afternoon to blue hour. The reflected foliage and lantern light must change with the actual scene. Check for a second silhouette along the bank and for white foam stuck to a moving camera.

Run `node references/check-creek.mjs` from the skill folder for the rock boundary, transmission, Fresnel and fall-distance checks. The [Momiji Creek demo](https://threeui.com/momiji-creek) shows the complete treatment.
