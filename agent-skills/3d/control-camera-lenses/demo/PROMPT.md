# Control Camera Lenses

## Minimal prompt

Use $control-camera-lenses to build a lens study with three clusters of autumn leaves at measured depths. Give me 24, 35, 58 and 85 mm lenses and independent focus and aperture controls. Keep the camera fixed when the focal length changes.

## Recreate the demo

Keep this study small enough to open from its own folder. Ship its renderer and assets locally. Set one metre as the unit of the scene. Give the viewer the controls needed to inspect the effect, with a visible label for each one. Use a textured foreground subject, a clear silhouette and enough distance behind it to judge the result at both extremes of the controls. Test the starting view before moving anything. Then change one control at a time and watch the effect from the same frame so that the result is easy to compare.

## Remix prompt

Move the study to the bank of an autumn creek. Use cool light from the open sky and warmer light from one side. Keep the existing controls working by keyboard. Show a still pose for readers who prefer reduced motion. Check the scene at a narrow viewport and at twice the usual display resolution.
