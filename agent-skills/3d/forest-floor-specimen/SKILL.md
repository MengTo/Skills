# Forest Floor Specimen

Give the specimen a closed, irregular soil core. Sink stones partially into the surface and leave exposed soil between moss cushions. Place every stem from sampled terrain height. Model a rising rachis, grow paired pinnae along its length, then add tapered leaflets. Reduce the spread toward the tip and vary the heights and rotations of neighboring fronds.

For a pinna, triangulate a narrow folded ribbon with a raised midrib. Give the upper and lower sides a slight color difference. Duplicate repeated leaflets using instancing or merge them by material. Drive wind with local stem height or a base-to-tip weight so the base remains pinned while the tip moves. Share a coherent gust per plant and add a smaller phase difference between fronds.

Use an orthographic camera for the editorial plate. Fit both width and height including the bend at maximum wind. Leave room for registration marks and a caption. Let scroll turn the group gently so thickness and overlap are visible. If an accordion switches studies, couple the mesh change, name and accessible expanded state.

Inspect from the side and below. Check the fern silhouette at phone scale, restore a still pose under reduced motion, and wake the renderer only while the specimen is visible or its selected stage has changed.
