Scatter stable points across the triangles in proportion to actual surface area. Sample each from a rest position and normal. Evaluate one brush function for the solid and the cloud. As the pointer holds over the surface, gradually clip the solid and lift nearby grains. Leave most of the grains hugging the silhouette and let only a sparse group drift into the surrounding air. On exit ease every grain home and reveal the entire solid. Preserve the hit target through the full handoff.
