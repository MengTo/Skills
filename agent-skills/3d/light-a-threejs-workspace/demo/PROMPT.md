# Light a Three.js workspace

## Minimal prompt

Use $light-a-threejs-workspace. Make an adjustable blind cast visible shafts across a procedural desk.

## Recreate the demo

Use $light-a-threejs-workspace. Build a window wall, walnut writing desk, monitor, mechanical keyboard, plant and desk lamp. Generate the grain and screen wallpaper locally. Use the same sun and slat spacing to cast shadows and integrate scattering in visible air up to the first opaque surface. Expose a Blind opening slider that changes both the shadows and the shafts.

Place the camera so the blinds, desk and monitor appear in the same frame. Show a matte painted wall behind the screen and give the wood consistent grain along its length. Close the blind while watching a sunlit patch on the desk. Slide the camera past the screen edge to check that the shafts stop at the first opaque surface.

## Remix prompt

Use $light-a-threejs-workspace. Move the window behind the monitor and turn the studio into a rainy evening reading room.
