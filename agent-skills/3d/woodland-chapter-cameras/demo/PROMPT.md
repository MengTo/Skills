# Woodland Chapter Cameras

## Minimal prompt

Use $woodland-chapter-cameras to build woodland chapter cameras for a botanical field guide.

## Recreate the demo

Build a standalone, responsive browser study of woodland chapter cameras for a fictional botanical field station named Alder. Stage a live Three.js valley with a creek, instanced trees and layered fog across a hero, story chapter and footer using distinct scroll-driven camera poses. Place varied instanced conifers around a winding watercourse on real terrain. Offer three camera stops and allow scroll to interpolate between them. Include reduced-motion handling, local dependencies and a legible phone layout. Test the interaction from a fresh load and after resizing to a narrow phone. Preserve a stable reading surface while decorative layers move. Inspect both the resting frame and the midpoint of the interaction, including focus visibility, readable contrast and graceful behavior when motion is reduced.

## Remix prompt

Adapt the study to a coastal field station. Preserve the interaction, readable hierarchy and keyboard behavior while changing the colors, shapes and editorial copy.
