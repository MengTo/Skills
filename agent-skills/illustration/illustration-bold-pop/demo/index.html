Line

Navy #1f1c4d outline 3.0 author units (2.34 px after the 0.78 card scale) on every shape, inner parts 2.2-2.6, marks 1.8, folds and finger contours 1.3-1.6, hatch 1.25 on a 5 grid; round caps and joins; extrusions 3-8 units stepped down-right.

Rules

  • One navy outline, 3.0 author units, on every shape
  • Every extrusion is solid navy and steps down-right
  • Flat saturated fills textured with navy hatch, dots, grids, stripes
  • Hatch the right or far side; light comes from the top-left
  • A slim 3/4 character beside a dense overlapping prop cluster
  • Scatter #, //, + and rings in the margins, never gradients
