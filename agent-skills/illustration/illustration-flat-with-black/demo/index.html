Line

No outlines on coloured fills; ink #15202f detail lines 0.73-1.6 (fingers 0.75, flaps 1.05, glasses 1.5), white seams 0.85-1.0 on black, ground line 1.25, round caps and joins.

Rules

  • No outline on any coloured shape; only white shapes get an ink edge.
  • Make hair, trousers, shoes or a device solid black.
  • Far limbs take the same hue one step darker.
  • Figures 5.2-5.5 heads tall with big shoes on the ground line.
  • Exactly two stars and two plus-sparkles, nothing else floats.
  • Centre the group; side margins near 90 px.
