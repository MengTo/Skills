Line

No stroke on any fill; white fold strokes 1.2-1.4; navy hairlines 0.8-1.4; sneaker outline 1.17; round caps and joins.

Rules

  • No outlines on big shapes; white fold strokes do all the shading.
  • Navy only for hair, sneakers, face features and a few hairlines.
  • Slim figures 205-235 px tall with 35 px heads and tapered legs.
  • One hero prop, in a colour the clothes don't use.
  • Six or seven confetti pieces; no ground line, no shadows.
