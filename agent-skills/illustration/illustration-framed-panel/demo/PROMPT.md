# Framed panel: prompts

## Minimal prompt

Use $illustration-framed-panel to draw a 480x360 SVG card of a product manager sitting on a stool sorting sticky notes on a board, for a "Roadmap" page.

## Recreate the demo

Use $illustration-framed-panel to draw three cards that sit next to `examples/support.svg`, `examples/design-system.svg` and `examples/live-coding.svg` as one set:

- Subjects: one design or coding job per card, each with one person mid-action (seated, kneeling or crouching), a wall prop inside the panel and two breakout pieces that tell the story (a question and an answer, a component and a type card, a chat bubble and a code snippet).
- Contract: `viewBox="0 0 480 360"`, a full-bleed pastel rect (peach, orchid or sky cyan), every id on its own prefix, presentation attributes only, at most three words of text. Start from `node scripts/panel-kit.mjs scaffold` and use `face()`, `sneaker()`, `bubble()`, `bush()` and the hand templates from `scripts/panel-kit.mjs`.
- Look: the fixed violet panel at 110,46 262x266 with its 1.35 navy frame, outline clouds, the dashed floor band, corner brackets, navy 1.25 outlines with paired hatch marks, two white breakouts across the frame edges and a bush crossing a bottom corner.
- Quality bar: 8/10 against a commercial illustration pack. Every hand passes the thumb proof, every foot has a contact ellipse, and the sheet next to the examples reads as one hand.

## Remix prompt

Use $illustration-framed-panel to draw "Code Review" on the orchid card: a reviewer sits sideways on a desk chair, leaning forward with one elbow on her knee and holding a tablet in both hands, a pull-request window with two diff rows hanging on the panel wall, a comment bubble with an avatar breaking out top-left and a green "Approved" chip card breaking out right. Keep the panel, frame, floor, brackets and bush exactly as the skill specifies; dress her in mint with tan skin and the bun hairstyle; keep the text to two words.
