Line

Filled brush polygons only (no stroke attributes): outlines swell 1-4.4 px with taper; details 1.1-3.2; hatching 1.0-1.15 at -48 to -58 deg, spacing 2-3.2.

Rules

  • Outlines are tapered brush strokes with one gap and one overshoot.
  • Fills slip 2-3.6 px past the line, with mottle and gloss.
  • Hatch shadows; never fill a dark area solid black.
  • Huge googly eyes, offset pupils, two specular dots each.
  • A critter or living object, never a person, on a grass patch.
  • Two to five ink hairs per stroke, nine to eleven flecks.
