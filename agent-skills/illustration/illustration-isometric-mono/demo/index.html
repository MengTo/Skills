Line

Outline #0f3d30 0.9 on object edges and silhouettes; 0.5-0.55 for insets and panels; 0.35-0.45 for tiny parts; detail lines 0.4-1.2 in ramp greens; round joins and caps.

Rules

  • True isometric: plan x and y at 30 degrees, z straight up, no vanishing points.
  • Fill each face by orientation: top lightest, left mid, right darkest.
  • Outline every edge in deep green at 0.9; details at 0.35-0.6.
  • Everything stands on one rounded, bevelled off-white slab.
  • Every object casts a soft shadow toward the lower right.
  • Seven to nine objects, tall ones at the back.
