Line

Indigo #3a2d66 stroke 1.4 round caps and joins on every filled shape; details 1 (folds, veins, brows), finger lines 0.9; background decor #cfc9e0 at 1, unfilled.

Rules

  • Fill shapes white; colour covers under 7% of the card.
  • One outline weight: 1.4 indigo, details 1, no shading.
  • Background decor in pale lavender line only: bricks, lamps, posters.
  • Floor line at y 308 with short dashes at both ends.
  • Slim realistic people, head about one seventh of height, calm faces.
  • The figure acts out the subject; plants and props frame it.
