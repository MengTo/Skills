# Outlined cartoon: prompts

## Minimal prompt
Use $illustration-outlined-cartoon to draw a 480 × 360 SVG card of a developer celebrating a green build.

## Recreate the demo
Use $illustration-outlined-cartoon to draw three new cards in the same set as `examples/`, one at a time:
- **Awards:** one figure in mid-leap, one fist raising a trophy with a `</>` on the cup, the other hand holding a laptop palm-up from below.
- **Code Review:** one figure lunging into a two-handed magnifier over a large pull-request card with diff rows, two check badges and a red comment bubble.
- **Designer:** one figure braced on a long roller pole, painting a green "Sign up" button, with a paint tray on the floor and taped colour swatches.

Contract: `viewBox="0 0 480 360"`, transparent background, one id prefix per card, no `<style>`, class, script or image. Build each card with a generator that imports `scripts/outline-kit.mjs`, so the three outline weights, the tapered folds and the union outlines match exactly. Quality bar: 8/10 against a commercial illustration pack on the five criteria in `references/craft.md`. Render a sheet next to the examples, zoom every hand at 8× with a thumb proof, and lint with `--palette style.json`.

## Remix prompt
Use $illustration-outlined-cartoon to draw "Ship Friday": a developer with brown skin and a dark blob afro, in a pink sweater and yellow trousers, pushing a big periwinkle "Deploy" button the size of a door with both palms, leaning in 15° with the back leg straight and the front knee bent. Keep the palette (periwinkle, tomato, yellow, leaf green, blush pink), the three ink weights (2.5 / 2 / 1.5 px), tapered fold slivers, thick brows, an open grin, one sparkle cluster at the top left, two rings, three dots and one rotated square. No ground line.
