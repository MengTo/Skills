Line

Ink #1e1b1b strokes with round caps and joins: 2.5 px silhouettes, 2 px hands and parts, 1.5 px inner details; folds are tapered filled slivers 1.1-2.4 px (brows 3.6-4); decor 1.6-2 px.

Rules

  • Outline every fill in warm near-black, three weights by role
  • Draw folds, brows and splits as tapered slivers, never strokes
  • Big head, huge white eyes, thick brows, open smile, blush
  • One character, one big hero prop, joined by the action
  • One sparkle cluster plus rings, dots and one square
  • Transparent card, no ground line, no shadows or gradients
