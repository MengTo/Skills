Rules

  • One hero object, no people, in the fixed -25/36 degree projection.
  • White top and front faces; teal only on right-facing sides.
  • Every shadow is hard, flat #0e4a48 and falls down-right.
  • Two to four floating extras, each with its own hard shadow.
  • Three deep-teal sparkles and up to three pale arc ticks.
  • Group centred, 58-90% of the card wide, 30 px margins.
