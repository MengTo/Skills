Palette

Line

Filled marker ribbons, never strokes: outlines 5.7-6.8 px, hands and small props 3.3-4.9, details and camo 2.2-3.1, floor 7.4-8.2; width wobbles ±34% with blob ends 26-78% wider.

Rules

  • Two inks only: black #141412 and mint #4fd592 on cream
  • Every line is a wobbly filled ribbon with blobby ends
  • Mint blocks slip 2-10 px off the outline
  • Solid black hair, trousers, shoes with mint highlight dashes
  • Crinkly squiggle camo only on off-white areas
  • Chunky profile people: big nose, dot eye, big hands and feet
