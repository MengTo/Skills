Palette

  • Cream#F8F0E3card, keyline, cream highlights, droplets punched into the ink
  • Ink#1F2B25outlines, shadow wedges, splash band, gulls, wordmark, tagline
  • Terracotta#C8693Fsun disc, one repeat on the hero
  • Sand#D9A873main fill of the hero
  • Tan#B5824Fdarker fill, shaded tone under sand
  • Pale#ECD2A8highlight streaks, rock faces, glass
  • Teal#3F8273one small accent: band, ring, collar or node dots

Line

Hero outlines 3.6 px ink #1f2b25 with round joins (inner parts 2.2-2.8), union outlines as a 7.2 ink underlay, a 15 px cream keyline stroke around every silhouette, no strokes on gulls or splash.

Rules

  • Seven flat colours on cream; teal only as one small accent
  • One chunky hero breaking a terracotta sun disc
  • Cut the hero free with a 7.5 px cream keyline
  • Shade with carved ink wedges, light with pale streaks
  • Slice the scene on a hard edge into an ink splash band
  • Heavy condensed wordmark over a small serif tagline
