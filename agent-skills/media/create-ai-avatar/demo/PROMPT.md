# Prompts for the demo

The demo is four presenters, each made from one portrait, talking on Avatar V, and one of them on all three engines with the mouths side by side. These prompts make your own.

## Make yours

> Use $create-ai-avatar to make my digital twin from the eye-contact takes of my last video. Say the price first. Then render "Hey, it's me. This is my digital twin, trained on my own takes, and speaking in my own ElevenLabs voice. Pretty wild, right?" in my voice on Avatar V, IV and III, and show me the mouths beside my real footage.

## Fix a twin that over-acts

> My avatar's mouth is too expressive. Render the same line, with the same audio and no motion prompt, on Avatar IV and Avatar III, and put the mouths beside my real takes.

## A photo instead of footage

> Use $create-ai-avatar to make this portrait a photo avatar and have it say this line in my ElevenLabs voice on Avatar V. No twin: just the photo and the audio.

## Remix the page

> Make a page like the skill's demo for my own avatars: a pick for each presenter and engine, the clip with a sound button, the mouth sheet with the picked row lit, and the price of each.

The demo's four presenters are photo avatars, each made from one portrait, speaking a 6 to 10 s line in a stock ElevenLabs v4 voice (Kristen, Hope, Cassidy and Mark).
