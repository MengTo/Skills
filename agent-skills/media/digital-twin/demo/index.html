The workflow

Eight stages, each with a gate

Price every run before it starts, log every spend, and put every result on one canvas with its recipe and your verdict.

  1. 0

    Consent and setup

    Only your own face and voice, or someone who gave documented consent. Accounts, a project folder, a canvas, a ledger.

    NeedsFil, the Higgsfield CLI, ElevenLabs, ffmpeg, whisper, Swift
    Gateconsent recorded; price before every spend
  2. 1

    References from your recordings

    Face crops from your own talking-head videos: a lean set of 8 (no smiling or mid-word frames), angle sets, 3 room photos, 5 held-out frames for the judge.

    Costfree · extract_frames.sh, faces.swift, measure.py
    Gatenever a selfie, a generated face or someone else’s frame
  3. 2

    The identity block

    One paragraph from a blind judge’s feature list. One fix, one phrase; say the near misses (“not a quiff”). Longer blocks over-correct.

    Costfree
    Gateunder 4,000 characters with the rest of the stack on Fil
  4. 3

    Stills, three at a time

    Identity → shared workspace → shot → gear and finish. Your real room with its photos last; only the camera and the time change.

    CostSeedream 5 Pro: 12 Fil ($0.12) or 2.5 Higgsfield (~$0.10)
    Gateyour pick; chin and width within ±0.05 n of your real frames
  5. 4

    Audit and fix

    Two fresh reviewers in 3×3 tiles. One edit lists every mistake; only the flagged regions are pasted back, with the face masked.

    Costan edit: 9 Fil or 2.5 Higgsfield · fix_still.py
    Gateno wrong gear, grey hair, extra objects, stands or mics
  6. 5

    Your voice

    An instant clone first. v4 with an audio tag if the pitch runs high. Score 7–10 s lines; then a before/after for your ear.

    CostElevenLabs characters · score_voice.py is free
    Gate≥ 9 out of 10; “sounds like me”
  7. 6

    Talking clips

    Seedance 2.5 draft from an audited close-up, the still as image reference too, your line as the audio reference, the soft-mouth clause.

    Cost15 Higgsfield (~$0.59) or ~52 Fil ($0.52)
    Gateone draft per still; English exact; mouth p50 near yours
  8. 7

    Deliver

    A canvas section per batch, notes on every node, picks marked and moved to a keep section, one question: which are you?

    Costfree
    Gateledger and log updated before you report

Voice

A score you can trust, then your ear

A speaker model (ECAPA-TDNN) compares each clip with your own clips, after the same band-pass and loudness chain, so a different mic can’t pass for a different person.

score = 0.7 × similarity + 0.3 × pitch
similarity: your held-out clips = 10, other people = 0
pitch: 10 inside your own p10–p90 range
  • 9 is you. One creator’s own voice, denoised, scored 9.3.
  • Score 7–10 s lines. A 4 s clip of the right voice scored 4.7.
  • Stability didn’t move v4’s pitch. The tag did, and whisper confirms it isn’t spoken.
  • Professional voice clones have a trap: adding samples resets verification, and the voice goes offline everywhere. You get five tries, then a 24-hour lock. Keep an instant clone as the stand-in.

What we learned

Ten rules, and the failure each one prevents

Use a lean reference set

Preventsa face 8–10 % long in the chin and 7 % wide. Seedream copies its references’ proportions, so smiling and mid-word frames stretch every image.

Your real room, only the camera changes

Preventsinvented lofts and cabins that read as fake, break continuity and, in that project, ran long in the chin.

Three at a time, numbered

Preventsbig batches nobody can judge. Three numbered shots make a pick one line: “144 145 are good”.

Angle references for every angle

Preventseye-contact and side shots drifting into someone else. Front frames gave the closest faces of all.

Reference videos as words only

Preventsanother person’s face blending into yours. Describe the composition and the light; never wire their frame in.

Words become features

Preventssilver hair from “grey scalp”, blue hair from “blue-black sheen”, a longer chin from “short chin”. One fix, one phrase.

Audit before video

Preventsan iMac, a ghost finger or a second laptop showing in every frame of a clip. Fix with one edit and paste back only those regions.

Calm mouth, calm head, one draft per still

PreventsSeedance’s 50 % too-wide mouth, a busy head, and near-duplicate drafts that waste money.

An instant clone and an audio tag

Preventsa clone that has your timbre but speaks 30 Hz high. [calm] on v4 scored 9.2; stability changed nothing.

Never add samples to a voice in use

Preventsa professional clone going offline on every model, then a 24-hour verification lock.

Consent

Only your own face and voice

A twin can say things the person never said. Build one only for yourself, or for someone who gave you documented consent for this exact use.

  • Never a public figure, a client, a friend “as a surprise”, anyone from photos found online, or a child.
  • Platform checks are the owner’s to do: the voice captcha, the phone face check. Never use a recording to pass one, and never click Skip.
  • Never work around a safety filter. If a check refuses something, stop and ask the owner.
  • Keep keys and samples private: API keys in a .env file, never in prompts, logs or screenshots.
  • Ask before publishing, and label generated media where a platform asks for it.
