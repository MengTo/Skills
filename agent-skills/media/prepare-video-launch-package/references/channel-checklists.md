# Channel checklists

Read only the requested channel sections. Verify current limits and policy against official sources before upload because platform rules change.

## Long-form video

- Confirm final master, title, description, chapters, thumbnail, captions, visibility, disclosures, and destination links.
- Recheck chapters against the uploaded file.
- Verify processing, checks, playback, and final URL before using it elsewhere.

## Short-form video

- Confirm aspect ratio, duration eligibility, safe areas, burned-in caption quality, cover frame, audio rights, visibility, and related long-form destination when supported.
- Do not call an ineligible upload a short-form post. Create a derivative only when authorized.

## Feed and professional-network video

- Inspect crop and caption readability in the real composer.
- Adapt the opening and context to the channel rather than pasting identical copy.
- Verify account identity, preview, links, disclosures, and scheduling state.

## Messaging or comment automation

- Match the trigger to the public call to action.
- Deliver only the promised resources.
- Keep placeholder URLs inactive.
- Activation, broadcasts, direct messages, and public replies each require explicit authorization.
