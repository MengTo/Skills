# Refract liquid glass

## Minimal prompt

Use $refract-liquid-glass. Build a draggable glass loupe over a landscape.

## Recreate the demo

Use $refract-liquid-glass. Paint a seeded pine shoreline, layered mountains and reflected sky into a local texture. Place a single rounded glass loupe over the horizon with crisp live lettering. Use an analytic edge normal and refraction to displace the same underlying landscape; borrow its color for the rim. Make the loupe draggable by touch and keyboard and let its spring settle after release.

Keep a thin high-contrast shoreline in the composition. Move the glass from bright sky over the ridge into dark water and reverse direction. Let the rim pick up local color throughout the drag. Keep the label legible at two device scales and make arrow keys move the lens when the grip has focus.
