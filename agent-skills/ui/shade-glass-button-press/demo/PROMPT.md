# Shade a glass button press

## Minimal prompt

Use $shade-glass-button-press. Make a glass toggle that responds to exactly where it is pressed.

## Recreate the demo

Use $shade-glass-button-press. Place a Change light button over a procedural coastal scene. On hold, indent the lens near the contact. On release, send an elliptic optical crest toward the far edge, splitting the sampled color only along the wave. Toggle between Daylight and Dusk once per activation. Support Space, Enter, cancellation and reduced motion.

Press near one corner and hold for half a second. The first highlight should stay near the contact until release, then sweep to the far edge. Try two quick taps and verify the second wave replaces the first one. Keep keyboard focus visible and offer a static held highlight under reduced motion.
