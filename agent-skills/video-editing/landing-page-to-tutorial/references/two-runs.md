# The two runs

## Greymourn (Nov 2026): a 4:30 film of a finished game site

The source of `making-of-film`. The site was built over days before any film was planned.

1. One reference video read shot by shot, plus channel research.
2. A facts file from the prompt log, then a claims check by a second reader: 9 of 53 claims were false.
3. A 720-word script.
4. Draft 1 on a free clock, with a stand-in voice and a drawn placeholder for the creator.
5. The cloned voice, then parallel threads: look and motion, real UI footage, backgrounds, the game's own plates.
6. Lines re-voiced, every phrase re-timed, judge rounds until every frame scored 8.
7. Other on-camera models tested and rejected; one avatar twin render, on camera for 26%, with no grade on the face.
8. The edit, the release package, and then the skill.

How it differed from a page tutorial: the footage came from the game's test hooks with a scripted player, there was
one look, and the story was about agents and blind judges. The released video was longer than the 4:30 film; how it
grew was not recorded.

## The design-tool tutorial (19 to 24 Nov 2026): three landing pages, 22:28

| Day | What happened |
|---|---|

| 23 Nov | Camera redone; cuts v11 to v19. |
| 24 Nov | Music by scene, covers, cut v22 at 22:28, 3840 × 2160, 60 fps. The pack: pages, prompts, an article, a description with 15 chapters. |

### The creator's notes and what each changed

**Designs**
- "put all of them, the concepts be in the same canvas, not each their own canvas" → one file.
- "Long scroll. Long scroll." → whole pages, not heroes.
- "show the exploration aspect… variants, copying, zooming, moving, dragging out a variant" → the exploring is filmed.
- "create multiple examples. More examples." → 75 designs.

**Script**
- v2: real build numbers in.
- v3, after "terrible": a 12 to 15 minute target, deeper.
- v4: rewritten in a speaking voice. v5: reactions. v6: a steering round.
- v7: "Give me three samples, 30s", and the plain, steady one was picked.
- v8: "I don't think we need to mention [the sponsor] in the first 20 seconds… start hooking people right away with
  something that they know"; "less emphasis on the scoring system and more on the models that I'm using and how much
  they cost"; type, colour, spacing and hierarchy; "how to judge an AI's output"; "Keep the length".
- v9: "I need to spend multiple hours polishing each of them. That's the final demo."

**Picture**
- "too many presentations… too few demos"; at least 25% real UI.
- "never clip designs… It needs to fill."
- "the background is the landing page that is scrolling, and the real landing page."
- "dont zoom in on the video. we use the original 16:9 video."
- "we don't need to transition all the time."
- No sponsor label in the hook.

**Camera**
- "I should be dressed accordingly to the scene" → dressed stills, approved.
- A first costume animation was rejected for likeness; a face composite read as a photo on a painting.
- "10 to 20% camera… About 25% showing a demo."
- "Concepts first, and then I'll approve the look."
- "In my normal attire. as close as possible to the camera."
- "fingers are wrong when i say a number."
- No camera on the end card.

**Music**
- "add music to different scenes of the video."
