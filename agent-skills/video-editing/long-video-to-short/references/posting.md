# Posting

Nothing here happens without the creator's typed yes for that exact thing: the captions, the time, turning a
schedule on. Nothing is posted or switched on without it; posts are saved paused.

## Two files, three posts

| Platform | File | Ends on |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | the first cut | "Comment WORD to get the prompts and the full video." |
| YouTube Shorts | the second cut | "So, check the full video in the description." |
| TikTok | the second cut | the same |

Before anything: the full video is **public** (not unlisted or scheduled) at the link the captions use. Check it
signed out.

Both cuts are under 30 MB at 60 fps, so they go up as they are. Any other video over 50 MB is compressed before
it's uploaded (H.264 for a post).

## Captions

- **Instagram.** First line is the ask ("Comment WORD and I'll send you …"). Then the short's point in two
  sentences, the steps as a numbered list someone could follow without the video, the sponsor line if the long video
  had one ("The full video is sponsored by X."), five hashtags. First comment: what the full video is and where the
  free resource is. No link in the caption; they don't work there.
- **YouTube Short.** A title under 60 characters that names the subject and the surprise. Description: the full
  video's link on the first line, the point in two sentences, the free resource's link, the sponsor line, hashtags
  ending in #Shorts.
- **TikTok.** Where the full video is, the point, the sponsor line, hashtags.

Name every tool and version exactly as the long video does; a bare "5.5" means nothing in a caption.

## Schedule, paused

Save the three posts at the chosen time but **off**. Show the creator the captions and the time, and turn them on
only on a clear "turn them on". If the time passes while they're paused, ask for a new one; never post late on your
own. TikTok may only accept an upload to drafts from a third-party tool: say so, and that they post it from the app.

## Labels the creator sets by hand

A twin on camera and a cloned voice are AI-made likeness, so the answer is yes on all three:

- Instagram: the AI info label on the reel.
- YouTube Studio: "Altered or synthetic content: Yes", once the Short is up.
- TikTok: the AI-generated switch when posting the draft.

No paid-partnership label unless this short itself was paid for.

## The comment keyword

After the Instagram reel is posted (the rule attaches to a post): keyword WORD, a public reply ("Sent. Check your
DMs."), a DM with the full video's link and the free resource. Save it off; they turn it on. Test it from a second
account before the reel gets reach.

## The long video's own post (X, Threads, LinkedIn)

Not the short: the post announcing the long video itself. One post per platform, never a thread; the live YouTube
cover as its picture and the video's youtu.be link as its only link. No free pack, site or tracking links (the
YouTube description carries those). For a sponsored video, X keeps its Paid partnership label and the sponsor is
tagged in the cover; Threads and LinkedIn say "sponsored by X" in the text. Positive only. Saved for the creator's
typed yes like the rest.
