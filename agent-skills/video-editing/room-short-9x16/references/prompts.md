# Prompts

Every prompt that made the first short built this way (24 Nov 2026), in the order they are used. Swap the bracketed parts and the
scene's own words; keep the rest. Stills are Seedream 5.0 Pro on Fil at 1.5k, 9:16 (6 credits a demo still, 8 with face
references). The look prompt goes on every still as a second prompt node wired into it, so one edit re-looks them all.

## 1. Storyboard frame (black and white, before any colour)

One frame per scene, labelled CAMERA or DEMO, with the line said over it. Approved as a sheet before any scene still.

## 2. The look and the scene stills

Camera stills (him) also take the creator's identity prompt and face references from `digital-twin`; demo stills take the
real page as an image reference so the screen shows the real design.

## 3. Seedance 2.5 draft, a line said to camera

Higgsfield CLI, `--mode omni_reference`, the approved still as both `--start-image` and `--image-references`, the line
in his voice as `--audio-references`, `--resolution 480p --draft true --aspect-ratio 9:16`, duration = the line rounded
up (4 to 7 s; 3 credits a second).

```
He speaks English, in his own voice from the audio reference, saying exactly: "[the line]" A locked-off [medium shot /
close-up] at his eye level; he sits [where, as in the still] and talks straight to the camera, relaxed and warm. His
head stays mostly still, with only small natural nods; his mouth moves softly, lips only slightly apart, no wide-open
mouth; brows low and relaxed, no startled look. [One gesture, tied to a word: "the other raises one finger on the word
'one'".] His face stays exactly as in the start image: the same man, clean, smooth, sharp, jet-black hair, no grey.
The dark room, the warm lamps and [what is behind him] stay exactly as in the start image. No camera movement, no
text, no captions, no music.
```

## 4. Seedance 2.5 draft, a working shot (no speech)

Same call without `--audio-references`.

```
He does not speak; his lips rest softly closed and do not form words. He types steadily on the white keyboard with
both hands, eyes on the screen, reading, one slow blink, a slight lean in. The camera makes a very slow push in, low
over the desk. The cool glow of the screen on his face shifts very slightly as the page changes. His face stays
exactly as in the start image: the same man, clean, smooth, sharp, jet-black hair, no grey, brows low and relaxed.
The dark room, the warm lamps and the window blinds stay exactly as in the start image. Only quiet keyboard clicks,
no speech, no music, no captions, no text.
```

The push-in is strong: by 5 s the keyboard is nearly out of frame, so place the caption on his shirt (y about 1120).

## 5. The 1080p final of a picked draft

Higgsfield MCP `generate_video` (the CLI's `--draft_job_id` fails): `{"model": "seedance_2_5", "draft_job_id": "<the
draft's job id>", "resolution": "1080p", "duration": <same>, "aspect_ratio": "9:16", "prompt": "<the same prompt>"}`.
16 credits a second. It keeps the take: the same words, timing, motion and sound (envelope correlation 0.99, no lag),
so the timeline and captions built on the draft stay valid.

## 6. Music bed (ElevenLabs Music on Fil, 60 s, 15 credits)

Written from the measured reference (75 bpm, bass-led, steady level, chords entering past the midpoint).

```
Instrumental only, no vocals. A calm, dark, late-night product film bed at 75 bpm in C minor. Deep round sub bass on
a slow half-time pulse, a soft muted kick and a light rim click, warm analog pads, and from the midpoint a gentle
glassy synth chord pattern that lifts a little without getting loud. Minimal, steady, confident and premium, like a
dim study lit by a laptop screen and warm lamps. Even level from the first second so a voice sits on top: no big
drops, no risers, no cymbal crashes, no melody that pulls attention. Starts straight on the beat with no long intro,
and ends on a soft held chord in the last two seconds.
```

## 7. The judge's brief

```
You are an independent judge of a finished [N] s vertical short (1080x1920, 60 fps). Judge only what you see; nobody
has told you it is good. File: [path]. Rubric and method: long-video-to-short/references/judging.md (frame strips cut
with ffmpeg, scores out of 10 per item with anchors). Context: [shots of him with times; what the demo shots are; how
the captions are set]. Section starts in seconds: [...].
Also check and report as facts: (1) any frame where caption text crosses his face or covers the page on the monitor;
(2) any caption word clipped by the frame edge or low-contrast against its background, with the time; (3) how jarring
each cut between the dark shots and the lighter room is; (4) sharpness of his shots against the renders; (5) anything
odd in his face or hands, with the time.
Return the scorecard (item, score, one-line reason), the single lowest item with exact times and a concrete fix, and
the facts for checks 1-5. Under 350 words.
```
