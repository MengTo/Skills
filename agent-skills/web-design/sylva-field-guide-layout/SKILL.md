# Sylva Field Guide Layout

Choose one responsive inset and max-width for the hero dock, copy, pricing cards and footer. Draw the continuous vertical guides on a separate pointer-transparent layer, slightly outside the inset of the readable content. Start with strokes at 10–17% opacity. Anchor a short diagonal or registration tick to an actual image or grid intersection.

Set display type in a light geometric sans such as Lexend. Use the same family for body, captions and navigation. Start display text around clamp(3rem,8vw,8.5rem), use near-solid leading and tune its tracking at the actual headline size. Keep labels 11px or larger and body copy around 16–18px. Limit the measure of prose independently of the display width.

Use a wide reading column and a narrower media or control column. Align chapter labels and folios to the same two edges, then interrupt the interior grid intentionally with a full-width landscape. On a phone, stack the figure after its text and collapse interior rules before reducing type. Reserve space for descenders and allow wrap at large text settings.

Check left-edge alignment across a light, dark and landscape section. Inspect at 320px and with enlarged text. A guide should disappear into scenery before it competes with a label.
